arithmetic-vs-harmonic VDOS-mean systematic that the single-frequency form hides.

  <files>src/qpd_potential/ia_broadening.py, tests/test_ia_broadening.py, GPD/phases/11-phonon-scale-conventions-and-ia-quantum-broadening-p-conv/11-02-IA-WIDTH-DERIVATION.md</files>
  <action>
Write the derivation out in full in `11-02-IA-WIDTH-DERIVATION.md`. Do **not** start from the closed
form. Start from the impulse approximation itself (Sears, PRB 35, 2038 (1987)): a nucleus struck with
momentum transfer `q` recoils freely, and the energy transfer is

    omega = q^2/(2 m_N) + q . p / m_N

where `p` is the nucleus's momentum **before** the collision. The first term is `E_R`. The second has
zero mean and variance `q^2 sigma_p^2 / m_N^2` for an isotropic momentum distribution of 1-D spread
`sigma_p`. With the zero-point spread `sigma_p^2 = m_N omega_bar / 2` and `q = sqrt(2 m_N E_R)`:

    sigma_E = q sigma_p / m_N = sqrt(2 m_N E_R) * sqrt(m_N omega_bar / 2) / m_N = sqrt(E_R * omega_bar)

Implement `sigma_E_eV(E_R_eV)` and `fractional_width(E_R_eV)` in
`src/qpd_potential/ia_broadening.py`, importing `omega_bar` from `params.py` (locked by plan 11-01,
CONVENTIONS.md Section J). Hard-code no phonon numbers.

**Flag the identity explicitly.** `sigma_E/E_R = sqrt(omega_bar/E_R) = 1/sqrt(2W)` is TRUE BY
CONSTRUCTION once `2W = E_R/omega_bar`. It is not evidence for the width and must not be reported as
a cross-check. Say so in the artifact. The decisive content of ROADMAP SC3 is the numerical values
