  <name>Task 1: Harmonic incoherent S(q,omega) from the frozen VDOS, and the sum rules</name>
  <files>src/qpd_potential/impulse_limit.py, tests/test_impulse_limit.py</files>
  <action>
Compute the harmonic **incoherent** dynamic structure factor from the frozen Ge VDOS by the
cumulant / self-correlation route. At `T -> 0`:

    gamma(t) = (q^2 hbar / (2 m_N)) * integral[ g(omega')/omega' * exp(-i omega' t) ] d(omega')
    2W       = gamma(0) = q^2 <u_x^2>
    S(q,omega) = (1/2pi) * integral[ exp(-i omega t) * exp(-2W + gamma(t)) ] dt

This is **one quadrature plus one FFT per q**. It is not a coherent-crystal backbone and not a
phonon-order expansion — both are out of scope. Cap the work at **5 q values**, corresponding to
`E_R` = 0.1, 0.5, 1, 10, 100 eV, with `q = sqrt(2 m_N E_R)`. If a clean FFT cannot be obtained inside
that cap (the large-`2W` cases oscillate fast), **stop and fall back to the analytic-moment route,
recording the fallback**. Do not buy convergence with scope.

Implement in `src/qpd_potential/impulse_limit.py`, reading the frozen VDOS from
`data/external/ge_vdos/` and `<u_x^2>` / `omega_bar` from `params.py`. Hard-code nothing.

Run the three exact checks first — they are the ones that catch implementation bugs independently of
any physics judgement:
- **Zeroth moment** `integral S d(omega) = 1`.
- **f-sum rule** `integral omega S d(omega) = q^2/(2 m_N) = E_R`. This is exact for **any**
  interaction, so a failure is a bug, full stop.
- **`gamma(0)` reduction** must return `2W = q^2<u_x^2>`, matching the CONVENTIONS.md Section J lock.
  A mismatch means the S(q,omega) quadrature and the Section J quadrature disagree, and one is wrong.

Then track the normalized width of `S` about `E_R` from 0.1 to 100 eV. Report whether the convergence
onto `delta(omega - E_R)` supports "the IA is reached by ~100 meV" **at the ~20% level or only at the
order-of-magnitude level**. Do not upgrade the verdict beyond what the numbers carry: `2W ~ 5` still
means a 35-46% fractional width.

Use `/opt/anaconda3/bin/python3` (numpy 1.26.4, scipy 1.17.1).
  </action>
