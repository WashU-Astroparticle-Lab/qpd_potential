<objective>
Fold the frozen CEvNS, muon, and Compton DEPOSITED-energy spectra through the
non-paralyzable response matrix R(E_rec | E_dep) for both trapping designs (Ta->Al, Al->Hf)
to produce the RECONSTRUCTED-energy differential-rate spectra (counts/kg/day/keV), with the
CEvNS recoil spectrum first rebinned onto the shared E_dep grid conserving counts.

Purpose: This is the core computation of the stage-1 deliverable (SIMU-03). It turns the
deposited-energy tracks into the decisive reconstructed-energy observable and produces the
per-design spectrum CSVs that Plan 06-02 plots. The fold is deterministic: the method is fully
fixed by Phases 3/4/5, so no researcher is needed.

Output: `src/qpd_potential/fold.py` (deliv-code), the two reconstructed-spectra CSVs
(inputs to deliv-fig-spectra), and `tests/test_fold.py`.
</objective>
