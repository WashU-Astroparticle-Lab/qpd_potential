<objective>
Re-declare the two v1.0 particle-background channels — cosmic-ray muons and the radiogenic
environmental-gamma Compton continuum — as the muon and gamma inputs of the unshielded
sea-level surface environment, **numerically identical to their committed values and verified
by direct comparison rather than by assertion**.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 9 SC1. Under the 2026-07-22 re-scope the background environment reverts
to the treatment `paper/sections/backgrounds.tex` already published and defended. Two of the
three channels are therefore not new work — they are already validated, and the risk is not
that they are wrong but that they get silently *changed* (renormalized, relocated, corrected
for a site) while being re-declared. This plan makes that impossible to do accidentally, and
records the direction each channel's accuracy errs so that the eventual S/B cannot quietly
inherit a stack of flattering choices.

Output: a provenance-headed two-channel declaration and an executable identity test module.
