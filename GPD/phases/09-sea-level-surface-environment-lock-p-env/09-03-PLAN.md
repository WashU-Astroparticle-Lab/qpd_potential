<objective>
Assemble the muon, gamma and neutron declarations into **one** frozen, provenance-headed
surface-environment input set, prove by execution that no shielded quantity entered it and that
every credit sentinel is exactly 1.0 by construction, and disclose for every channel the
direction its central value errs relative to its own anchor.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 9 SC1 and SC5, and the phase's deliverable line — a single environment
declaration that Phases 13, 14 and 15 all draw from. The directional-bias audit is this phase's
structural response to the Phase-8 failure mode: three of the four errors found there pointed in
the direction that favoured the expected conclusion, and the fourth surfaced only when a verifier
was explicitly told to hunt for one. An input set assembled from three separately-chosen central
values can accumulate that bias invisibly; this plan makes the accumulation visible in the
artifact, before any signal-to-background number is computed on top of it.

Output: the environment registry, the declaration document, the loader module, the test module,
and a researcher checkpoint on the completed audit.
</objective>
