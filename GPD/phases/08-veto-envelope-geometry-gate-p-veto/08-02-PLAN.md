Determine what muon rejection the wafer earns from its own geometry, and separate it cleanly from the rejection it cannot earn.

Purpose: Success Criteria 3 and 4. NUCLEUS's headline veto numbers are payload-coupled and may not be transferred; the honest replacement is the rejection the wafer derives from its own chord-length distribution. The decisive subtlety is that NUCLEUS's greater-than-99.8-percent figure is a muon-INDUCED background rejection, not a muon tagging efficiency — so the wafer's near-perfect ability to tag muons that cross it says nothing about the neutrons and bremsstrahlung gammas born in the surrounding lead. Reporting one number instead of two would convert that distinction into an inherited veto credit.

Output: `src/qpd_potential/wafer_self_veto.py` with the two acceptances as separately named quantities, the multiplicity identity, and the live-time cost; a provenance-headed `data/wafer_self_veto.csv`; and unit tests asserting the normalization closure, the acceptance limits, and the multiplicity identity.
</objective>
