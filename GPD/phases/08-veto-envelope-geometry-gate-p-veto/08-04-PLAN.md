<objective>
Fix, in writing and in code, which of NUCLEUS's rejection statements transfer to the wafer and which do not.

Purpose: Success Criterion 2, and the deliverable that survives the gate verdict either way. Phase 16 must report an L2-off baseline, and it can only do that if the classification is binding and the credit is structurally hard to undo. Three things make this more than a table. NUCLEUS section 5.2.1 contains three distinct factor-5 statements and the project's own notes quote a bare number without saying which.
