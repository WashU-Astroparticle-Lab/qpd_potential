<objective>
Make out-of-domain evaluation of every tabulated-input interpolator an error instead of a
silent clamp, slope-extrapolation, or zero. This is ROADMAP Phase 10 success criterion 3,
and it must land before the grid is extended, because extending the deposit axis two
decades below 10.14 eV is exactly the operation that will push evaluations below table
floors.

Purpose: the guard against a kinematic threshold silently falling below a tabulated floor
and yielding zero instead of an error. Without it, the sub-eV extension can produce a
clean-looking spectrum that is an artefact of clamping.
Output: a shared bounds-checking helper, guarded call sites, boundary unit tests at each
table's real floor and ceiling, and a reproducible inventory of every interpolation call
site with a guarded/excluded verdict.
</objective>
