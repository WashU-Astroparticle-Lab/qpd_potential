## Overview

v2.0 answers a single question, restated 2026-07-22 for an unshielded surface wafer: **what signal-to-background does a ~110 g QPD Ge wafer achieve as an unshielded surface detector, in reconstructed energy, down to 100 meV?** The background side reverts to the treatment the v1.0 manuscript already published and defended (`paper/sections/backgrounds.tex`): sea-level Gaisser–Guan muon flux folded through the ray-box chord distribution, plus the radiogenic environmental-gamma Compton continuum — no overburden, no passive shielding, no veto. Two things are added to that treatment. First, a **rough sea-level neutron estimate**, because neutrons were ~91% of NUCLEUS's shielded RoI budget and the v1.0 paper omitted them entirely; dropping them without comment would flatter the result. Second, the sub-eV machinery this milestone was built to develop — the 0.1 eV grid, the trigger-probability observable, and impulse-approximation quantum broadening — which is site-independent and survives the re-scope untouched.

Nine phases (8–16), of which Phase 8 is complete (gate: NO FIT) and Phases 10 and 11 are unchanged from the pre-re-scope roadmap because the Phase-8 verifier established that neither carries a single NUCLEUS anchor. The signal keeps **the paper's own 3 GW_th / 25 m surface scenario as primary** — ∫Φ = 7.5×10¹² ν̄/cm²/s, frozen in `data/flux/reactor_flux_v1.0.csv`, already verified, and already the `CONVENTIONS.md` §D lock. That makes the primary configuration **one coherent physical situation**: an unshielded surface wafer 25 m from a 3 GW_th core, signal and backgrounds in the same place. The NUCLEUS VNS siting enters only as an **optional secondary line** — a labelled scalar rescale of the primary result by the flux ratio 2.1×10¹² / 7.5×10¹² ≈ 0.28, quoted for context, never a separate pipeline run. **Accuracy expectation is explicitly lowered** across the re-scoped phases (user, 2026-07-22): bounded estimates and closed-form folds, not regularized inversion, curve-digitization campaigns, or multi-target over-determination. No phase carries an OpenMC, MCNP, Geant4, or G4CMP dependency: OpenMC is unbuildable on osx-arm64 and full transport is out of scope by explicit user decision.
