# Billard (2017) Table-1 Reproduction — VALD-01

**Phase 3, Plan 03-02.** Source: J. Billard et al., J. Phys. G **44**, 105101 (2017),
arXiv:1612.09035, Table 1 + assumptions. Reproduces the natural-Ge CEvNS reactor
rates 0.76 / 0.51 / 0.26 counts·kg⁻¹·day⁻¹ above 50 / 100 / 200 eV_nr by folding
the frozen Billard-*variant* flux (`data/flux/reactor_flux_billard_variant.csv`)
through the LOCKED 03-01 per-isotope dσ/dT, after the explicit geometry+power
renormalization **k ≈ 0.0111**.

Generated by `cevns.reproduce_billard()` / `cevns.billard_k_factor()`
(n = 2000-point log-T fold). All rates are DEPOSITED nuclear-recoil energy
(eV_nr); no quenching, no detector response (Phase 5).

## 1. The renormalization factor k

The `billard_variant.csv` carries Billard's **spectral shape** (Huber–Mueller held
constant below 2 MeV; fission fractions 55.6/32.6/7.1/4.7; no n-capture) but at
**our** 3 GW_th / 25 m normalization (∫Φ dE = 4.586×10¹²). Billard's Table 1 is at
Chooz's **8.54 GW_th combined at ~400 m** (two cores at 355.39 & 468.76 m, 4.27 GW
each). The variant must be rescaled by

    k = (P_B / P_v) · (G_B / G_v),   G = point-source 1/(4π d²) geometry factor.

Both powers are THERMAL (GW_th, **not** GW_e); the 4π cancels in the ratio so only
(d_v/d_B)² enters. Evaluated two ways:

| Treatment | Formula | k |
|---|---|---|
| Single source (caption: 8.54 GW @ 400 m) | (8.54/3)·(25/400)² | **0.0111198** |
| Two cores (text: 4.27 GW @ 355.39 & 468.76 m) | [4.27/355.39² + 4.27/468.76²]/[3/25²] | **0.0110917** |

The two geometry treatments agree to **0.25 %** (< 1 %). The k-rescaled Billard
integral flux is **5.10×10¹⁰ ν cm⁻² s⁻¹** (expected ≈ 5.1×10¹⁰) — the correct
Chooz-at-400 m magnitude.

**Forbidden proxies rejected here:** `fp-gwe-gwth` (both powers thermal; distance
enters squared — a 1/d error would give k ≈ 0.18, a GW_e error k ≈ 0.004; a guard
test brackets k ∈ [0.006, 0.05]).

## 2. Reproduced rates vs Billard Table 1 (WITH k)

| Threshold | Billard Table 1 | This work (k-rescaled) | Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| > 50 eV_nr  | 0.76 | **0.7415** | −2.4 % |
| > 100 eV_nr | 0.51 | **0.5009** | −1.8 % |
| > 200 eV_nr | 0.26 | **0.2567** | −1.3 % |

All three bins reproduce Table 1 to **< 2.5 %**, well inside the ~20 % VALD-01
tolerance. The 100 / 200 eV bins (insensitive to the sub-2-MeV shape) agree to
< 2 %, so the **ROADMAP backtrack trigger (100/200 eV off > 20 %) is NOT met** — no
cross-section-convention backtrack. The 50 eV bin, most sensitive to the residual
sub-2-MeV shape (our HM-flat variant vs Billard's exact low-E treatment), shows the
largest deviation as anticipated, but still only −2.4 %.

**Per-isotope Ge sum used** (Z=32; A=70/72/73/74/76; x=0.2057/0.2745/0.0775/0.3650/
0.0773), NOT a lumped A=72.63 (`fp-lumped-A-billard` rejected).

## 3. The ~90× overshoot WITHOUT k (fp-billard-norm regression)

Folding the variant at its **stored** 3 GW_th/25 m normalization (i.e. k = 1) and
comparing to Table 1 is the forbidden proxy `fp-billard-norm`:

| Threshold | Without k (stored norm) | Billard | Overshoot |
|---|---|---|---|
| > 50 eV_nr  | 66.68 | 0.76 | **89.9×** |
| > 100 eV_nr | 45.05 | 0.51 | **89.9×** |
| > 200 eV_nr | 23.09 | 0.26 | **89.9×** |

The overshoot is a uniform **89.9×** = 1/k — exactly the geometry+power ratio
between our 3 GW_th/25 m config and Billard's 8.54 GW/400 m, confirming the mismatch
is purely normalization (spectral shape identical). This is guarded by
`test_without_k_overshoots_90x`.

## 4. CONUS+ coarse factor-~2 cross-check

Secondary external anchor: CONUS+ (Nature **643**, 1229 (2025), arXiv:2501.05206),
Ge CEvNS at 3.6 GW_th / 20.7 m. Rescaling BOTH the flagship (flux v1.0, 3 GW_th/25 m,
∫Φ = 7.5×10¹²) and Billard's Table-1 rate to the CONUS+ config by (P/P′)(d′/d)²:

| Quantity | Value (> 50 eV_nr) |
|---|---|
| Flagship rate (our config) | 67.75 counts/kg/day |
| Flagship → CONUS+ config | 118.6 counts/kg/day |
| Billard Table 1 → CONUS+ config | 119.6 counts/kg/day |
| **Ratio** | **0.99** (within factor ~2) ✓ |

Two **independently-normalized** flux models (our HM+summation+n-capture flagship
vs Billard's HM-flat variant) predict the same Ge CEvNS scale at a common config to
1 %. **CAVEAT:** CONUS+ reports eV_ee (ionization scale) and requires a quenching
model to convert to our deposited-eV_nr phonon scale, so this is a **coarse
rate-scale check only, NOT a direct spectrum match**.

## 5. Assumptions used (matched to Billard)

- Huber–Mueller spectra held **constant below 2 MeV** (encoded in the variant CSV).
- Fission fractions 235U/239Pu/238U/241Pu = 55.6/32.6/7.1/4.7 %.
- **No** n-capture (excluded in the variant, per Billard).
- Per-isotope natural-Ge sum; cross section = LOCKED 03-01 Freedman dσ/dT with
  /4π prefactor, Q_W = N − (1−4sin²θ_W)Z, sin²θ_W = 0.2387, Helm F(q), (ħc)².
- ±5 % flux normalization (Billard's stated flux uncertainty).

## 6. Confidence

**HIGH.** Reproduction < 2.5 % across all three bins via an independently derived,
two-way-cross-checked k; the without-k overshoot equals 1/k exactly; CONUS+ ratio
0.99. Weakest anchor: the residual sub-2-MeV shape (our HM-flat vs Billard's exact
low-E) is unverified, but it only touches the 50 eV bin and the deviation there is
−2.4 %.
