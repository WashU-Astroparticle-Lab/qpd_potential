## 2. Per-channel physics chain and provenance

### 2.1 Muon — re-declared, numerically identical to v1.0

Sea-level intensity from the **modified-Gaisser ("Gaisser–Guan")** parametrization with the
effective zenith angle cos θ\* (P1–P5 = 0.102573, −0.068287, 0.958633, 0.0407253, 0.817285),
folded with the **ray–box chord distribution** of the 10.16 × 10.16 × 0.20 cm slab under the
inward-surface-projection measure I(θ)·A_proj·sin θ, folded with the **Landau–Vavilov** density
whose mode is the **most-probable value Δ_p**. The mean deposit and the Moyal approximation are
**not** used. Matches `paper/sections/backgrounds.tex` §"Cosmic muons".

Re-derived from the committed modules (not restated from the CSV): ⟨ℓ⟩ = 4V/S = **0.384848 cm**
(−0.039 %), ξ(vertical) = **0.072069 MeV** (−0.043 %), Δ_p = **1.230614 MeV** (−0.137 %), strictly
below ⟨Δ⟩ = **1.458502 MeV**; κ = ξ/T_max = 6.73×10⁻⁵ (deep Landau regime).

### 2.2 Gamma — re-declared, numerically identical to v1.0

**Bound-electron incoherent** scattering: Klein–Nishina dσ/dΩ × **Hubbell (1975) S(x, Z = 32)** at
x = E_γ[keV]·sin(θ/2)/12.39842 Å⁻¹, sampled on the pinned Cauchy mean chord ℓ̄ = 0.384848 cm in
the optically thin wafer (μℓ̄ = 0.117382 at 1 MeV, double-scatter 1.3778 %). The deposit is the
recoil-electron kinetic energy T_e = E_γ − E′, a **continuum, not a photopeak**. Matches
`backgrounds.tex` §"Environmental gammas".

Compton edges recomputed in closed form from `data/gamma_lines.csv`: ⁴⁰K **1243.3573 keV**,
²¹⁴Bi **1541.3115 keV**, ²⁰⁸Tl **2381.7571 keV** — all within 0.05 keV of the committed values.
Zero non-zero content in any bin whose *lower* edge exceeds the highest edge, and exactly 0.0 at
the 2614.511 keV line. **The rate of record is 2.6747×10⁻¹ Hz (bound incoherent)** and is kept
distinct from the free-KN pre-binding 2.6846×10⁻¹, the VALD-03 anchor 2.7305×10⁻¹, and the
paper's rounded 0.267.

### 2.3 Neutron — the one genuinely new input

**The v1.0 paper omitted neutrons entirely.** This set adds them. PARMA v4.10 / Sato-2015 supplies
the differential **shape**; Gordon-2004 supplies the **>10 MeV integral anchor only** — its
differential coefficients are **paywalled** and were unsourceable, so it is never the shape
source. Coefficients were taken from the official PARMA C++ source at pinned commit
`6ff37cacb8cf003e2fc269963f9a08f812407264`, compiled **unmodified**, and not typed.

**Phase-7 verification gap D2 is closed.** A driver is now committed
(`src/qpd_potential/parma_neutron_flux.py`) and every decisive number is recomputable in-repo:
untuned Φ(10 MeV–10 GeV) = 3.238744×10⁻³ (−0.008 % vs the committed native value, and **−8.77 %
vs Gordon with no fitting** — the only independent validation this channel has, reported *before*
the rescale); anchored Φ = 3.549986×10⁻³ (−0.0004 %); independently solved k = 1.096104
(+0.0004 %); Φ(0.01 eV–10 GeV) = 1.317379×10⁻² (+0.029 %). The **joint identity** D2 said had
never been performed — driver vs the committed table on its own 584 bin centres — gives median
**and** max relative deviation **3.88×10⁻⁶**, which is exactly the rounding of k.

The **lethargy division by E happens exactly once**, inside PARMA's own `getNeutSpecCpp`
(`subroutines.cpp:673`), proved by ∫φ dE = ∫(Eφ) d(ln E) to <0.0001 % on five decades.

**Band semantics.** `phi_default` = `phi_hi` (**outdoor**) is operative. `phi_lo` =
`phi_default`/5 describes **indoor/building attenuation** and is **not an error bar for this
configuration**; it is not used and not emitted.

## 5. Directional-bias audit

**Why this audit exists.** In Phase 8, **three of the four errors found pointed in the direction
that favoured the expected conclusion**, and the fourth surfaced only when a verifier was
explicitly told to hunt for one. An input set assembled from three separately-chosen central
values can accumulate that bias invisibly. This table makes the accumulation visible **in the
artifact, before any S/B number is computed on top of it.**

| channel | central value | anchor | **signed deviation** | **direction** | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **muon** | 1.3659 Hz | PDG sea level, **Leg A**: ≈1 muon cm⁻² min⁻¹ × A_top (103.2256 cm²) = **1.7204 Hz** | **−20.61 %** | **`flatters_SB`** | The adopted rate is **below** the primary anchor, so the muon background is under-counted; less background raises S/B. |
| **gamma** | 2.6747×10⁻¹ Hz | the **LABChico measured** normalization — the adopted value **is** the anchor; factor-2 site band around it | **+0.00 %** | **`neutral`** | The committed normalization sits at the measured anchor, i.e. the **centre** of its factor-2 band, not on either edge. The **low** edge (×0.5 → 0.1337 Hz) is the flattering one and is **not** used; the high edge (×2 → 0.5349 Hz) would penalize. Band carried **unnarrowed**. |
| **neutron** | Φ(10 MeV–10 GeV) = 3.550×10⁻³ cm⁻²s⁻¹ on `phi_default` = `phi_hi` (**outdoor**) | Gordon 2004 NYC, 3.5–3.6×10⁻³, **midpoint** adopted | **+0.00 %** anchored by construction; the **untuned** PARMA prediction sits at **−8.77 %** | **`penalizes_SB`** | The channel adopts the **outdoor/upper** band leg and the Gordon **midpoint**, not the low edge; and the NYC evaluation point is *low* for a higher-altitude or higher-cutoff site, so the true normalization would move **up** → more background → lower S/B. This choice **costs** S/B rather than buying it. |

**Channels on the flattering side of their own anchor: 1 of 3** (muon). Not all three — so the
adverse reading of §"competing explanations" (a systematic selection pressure across the whole
input set) is **not** what the evidence shows here. The reading being invoked is the benign one,
and it is invoked **because the audit came out mixed**, not in spite of it.

### 5.1 Compounded effect — the number

*If every flattering choice were replaced by its anchor value, by what factor would the total
declared background change?*

The only common currency that exists **at this phase** is the through-wafer **interaction rate**
of the two channels that have one. The neutron channel has **no rate yet** — it is an incident
flux in cm⁻² s⁻¹ and acquires a rate only after the Phase-13 fold — so it **cannot** enter this
factor and is reported separately below rather than silently folded in.

| | muon [Hz] | gamma [Hz] | total [Hz] |
|---|---|---|---|
| as declared | 1.3659 | 0.26747 | **1.63337** |
| every flattering choice → its anchor | 1.7204 | 0.26747 | **1.98787** |

> ### **Compounded-effect factor = 1.2171** — i.e. **the total declared background would rise by 21.7 %**, and S/B would fall by the same factor, if the one flattering choice were replaced by its anchor.

**Reported alongside, because the audit must not overclaim its own anchor.** The two PDG statements
`ref-pdg-muon` quotes are not mutually consistent at the ±20 % level and **bracket** the adopted
value: Leg A (≈1 muon cm⁻² min⁻¹) → 1.7204 Hz, **−20.61 %**; Leg B (I_v ≈ 70 m⁻²s⁻¹sr⁻¹ with
I ∝ cos²θ, J = πI_v/2) → 1.1350 Hz, **+20.34 %**. On **Leg B** the muon channel would be
`penalizes_SB`, no channel would sit on the flattering side, and the compounded-effect factor would
be **0.8586** (background *falls* 14.1 %). Adding the wafer's side-entry faces raises either anchor
by only **+1.97 %** under a cos²θ-weighted projected-area integral, so side entry does not resolve
the discrepancy. **The label `flatters_SB` is assigned on the anchor leg the v1.0 paper itself
invokes**, and the honest magnitude of the muon channel's directional bias is **≲20 % in either
direction**, dominated by the 30–35 % Gaisser–Guan spread no in-repo artifact can narrow.

## 6. What is **NOT** in this set

- **Muon-induced neutron production** in the wafer and its housing. It is in **neither** channel:
  the sea-level ambient neutron spectrum declared here is a **cosmic-ray secondary field treated
  as an incident flux**, and no in-detector production is included in either the muon or the
  neutron channel. This is recorded as an **omission, not as a negligible quantity** — nothing in
  this phase bounds it. Attributing muon-induced neutrons to the ambient field as well as to the
  muon channel would double-count them; omitting them entirely, as here, under-counts. Neither
  error is committed silently.
- The **>20 MeV σ_el** region (Phase-7 gap D1; truncate-and-document by user decision 2026-07-22).
- The **>197 MeV flux tail**, holding 21 % of the >10 MeV flux. Note that CALC-24's Phase-7
  deferral rationale — that shield attenuation made the >10 MeV tail unimportant — is VOID at
  the surface (NOT applied).
- **Any veto credit whatsoever.** There is no veto; the credit is 1.0 by construction.
- Any **fold, response matrix, recoil spectrum, or reconstructed-energy quantity** (Phases 13–15).
- Any **grid extension** below 10.14 eV on the project shared grid (**Phase 10**, running in
  parallel).
- Any **NUCLEUS VNS pipeline, flux table or spectral shape** (`fp-second-vns-run`). The VNS
  survives only as a labelled scalar rescale (~0.28) of a finished primary result.

## 7. The weakest anchor, stated without softening

**The neutron channel's spectral shape has no independent validation.** Gordon's differential
coefficients are paywalled, so the only cross-check that exists is a **single integral above
10 MeV agreeing to ~8.8 % untuned**. Two spectra can share that integral and differ badly at the
**eV–keV** energies that actually set the Ge recoil rate in the CEvNS region of interest. That is
why the channel is `order_of_magnitude`, and why **no downstream acceptance test may demand better
than order-of-magnitude agreement on it.**

Behind it: the absolute environmental-gamma normalization (factor-2 site band, with the
²³⁸U-chain contribution set by an **assumed chain balance** rather than a measured line
intensity), and the Gaisser–Guan absolute muon normalization (30–35 % inter-experiment spread,
named in the v1.0 paper itself as the weakest anchor of that channel).

**The one configuration question this phase cannot settle:** whether the wafer is genuinely
**outdoors**. It moves the neutron flux by a factor ~5 and reshapes it non-uniformly
(building moderation shifts fast → thermal). It is a **configuration statement, not an uncertainty
band**, and no scalar can represent it.
