## Axis discipline (applies to ALL three inputs) — CONVENTIONS.md §B

Every locked input is tagged **keV_nr on the unified phonon energy scale** (T ≡ E_nr = E_dep), for BOTH
nuclear-recoil (neutron elastic, CEvNS) and electron-recoil (γ Compton, β/EC) deposits.

**§B FORBIDDEN guard (verbatim intent):**
- **No keVee ↔ keVnr scale mixing.** A keVee-quoted number placed on the phonon axis lands ~5× low.
- **No Lindhard / ionization-quenching (QF) factor** applied on this phonon scale. Applying a
  QF ≈ 0.1–0.3 would mis-normalize dR/dE by ~1/QF ≈ 4–6×. QF is used ONLY to *un-quench* an imported
  keVee number into keV_nr, never to quench a number already on the phonon scale.

**Surface-vs-underground non-transferability guard (Pitfall 2):**
- The surface baseline uses the raw sea-level flux with **NO shielding factor**.
- Shielded / underground residuals — **NUCLEUS (~250 dru), CONUS, RELICS** — are **post-shield residuals,
  NOT the unshielded surface flux**. They are back-corrected cross-checks only (VALD-08, Phase 12). The
  surface baseline is set as an *upper* case relative to them. Importing any such residual as the surface
  baseline (guard **fp-underground-import**) is forbidden.

**Why BLOCKED.** The plan requires the differential spectral **shape** φ(E_n) to be built from the
Gordon-2004 / JEDEC JESD89A analytic fit coefficients, **sourced or digitized at execution** and never
reconstructed from memory. In this environment:
- The IEEE paper is paywalled; JESD89A is registration-gated; the ADS record is JS-rendered (no
  coefficients); scripted retrieval of the coefficient table failed.
- The prior phase-7 literature survey (`07-RESEARCH.md` → "What Was NOT Found") *already* recorded a
  thorough web survey (ResearchGate, ADS, Semantic Scholar) that **did not surface the coefficient table**.
- The published figure cannot be fetched/viewed here, so the digitization fallback is not defensibly
  executable either.

The two **integrals** (3.5×10⁻³ >10 MeV; ~1.3×10⁻² broad) are defensibly cited, but they do **not**
uniquely fix the differential shape (thermal Maxwellian peak, 1/E epithermal region, ~1–2 MeV
evaporation hump, high-E cascade tail) that drives the downstream recoil spectrum. Building φ(E_n) from
memory-based component forms would be exactly the fabrication the plan forbids (guard: improvising a
normalization). Per the plan `stop_and_rethink` condition, this input is **BLOCKED pending scope repair**.

**Resolution requested (any one):** (a) the Gordon-2004 / JESD89A analytic coefficient set; (b) a digitized
(E_n, φ) table from the published Gordon spectrum figure, with the digitization source; or (c) authorization
to represent φ(E_n) as an explicitly-labeled physically-motivated multi-component parametrization
**normalized to the two cited Gordon integrals**, carried as an approximation with its own uncertainty
(this is a genuine scope change, not the plan's current "sourced coefficients" requirement).

`data/ambient_neutron_flux_v1.1.csv` is therefore **not written** — no fabricated spectrum is committed.

improvise a normalization** — whenever:

1. **A gating input cannot be pinned to a defensible cited default + band.** *Currently TRIGGERED for

   open bracket → non-predictive CALC-07/08). *Not triggered* — a representative middle is forced (§2).

   the committed φ(E_n) misses Φ(>10 MeV)=3.5×10⁻³ beyond cited precision; any locked number carries a
   0.1–0.3 quenching factor; an NR background peaks ~5× low.

integral-normalized parametrization as a scope change) unblocks `data/ambient_neutron_flux_v1.1.csv` and
