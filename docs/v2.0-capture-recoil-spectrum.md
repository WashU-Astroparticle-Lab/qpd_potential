# The (n,gamma) capture nuclear-recoil spectrum

**Written:** 2026-07-23 · Replaces the shapeless hatched band on the combined figure with a
derived spectrum for the resolved cascades. Grounded entirely in **locally frozen sources**.

## Why this channel had no shape

Prompt (n,gamma) capture is **44% of the 10-100 eV RoI background** (43.998 counts/kg/day after
the assumed /100 suppression). Phase 14 could only deliver an **integrated bound** — R_RoI ≤
R_capture = 4399.78 counts/kg/day — because a recoil spectrum needs the gamma cascade, and the
in-repo EGAF line lists carry only **60-71%** of the capture Q-value (the pandemonium problem).

## This is a known problem with published tools

Neutron-capture recoils are the subject of a dedicated literature for sub-keV Ge detectors, now
frozen locally:

- **Biffl, Gevorgian, Harris & Villano**, *Neutron capture-induced nuclear recoils as background
  for CEvNS measurements at reactors*, PRD **107**, 092011 (2023), arXiv:2212.14148 —
  `data/external/biffl/2212.14148v2.pdf` (sha256 `5eee50ee…6639c`). This is the published
  treatment of exactly our background. It computes the recoil spectrum with **`nrCascadeSim`**.
- **Villano, Harris, Brown**, *nrCascadeSim*, J. Open Source Software **7**, 3993 (2022),
  arXiv:2104.02742, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.5579857 — the dedicated public code. Its curated
  natural-Ge cascade tables are frozen at `data/external/nrcascadesim/` (repo commit `0f7793e`).

Capture recoils are also used as an experimental **calibration source** in Ge, reported near
254 eV_nr — so this channel has an external anchor near our RoI. (Those measurement papers are
not yet retrieved; the 254 eV value is unverified pending a freeze.)

## What we compute, and what we do not

`nrCascadeSim` requires ROOT, which this project does not add (the OpenMC/Geant4 line). We use its
**data** — the curated cascade level/lifetime tables, which are the hard nuclear-structure input —
plus its **Weisskopf coefficients read from `src/weisskopf.cpp`**, not recalled. The recoil
kinematics are elementary and implemented directly in `src/qpd_potential/capture_recoil.py`:

    gamma i carries momentum p_i = E_i / c; the nucleus takes -p_i.
    T = |sum of momenta since the ion last stopped|^2 / (2 M c^2)

The one thing not reproduced is their continuous constant-stopping-power slowing, which governs
how much momentum survives between emissions. It is **bracketed**, not approximated:

| mode | assumption | recoil |
|---|---|---|
| `slow` | ion stops between every emission | `T = Σ E_i² / 2Mc²` (a line per cascade) |
| `fast` | ion never slows | `T = \|Σ p⃗_i\|² / 2Mc²` (continuous) |
| `lifetime` | binary: stopped if sampled `t ~ Exp(τ)` exceeds `τ_stop` | between the two |

The true spectrum lies between `slow` and `fast` **by construction**, since partial slowing
interpolates the two limits.

## Results

Validated:
- **Energy closure: 0/74 cascades** violate ΣE_γ = S_n (S_n from ENDF `MF=3 MT=102 QM`,
  cross-checked against the EGAF level schemes: 73Ge→74Ge 10.1962 MeV vs 10196.1 keV).
- **Mean recoil = 321 eV in all three modes** (agree to 0.03%): the identity
  ⟨\|Σp⃗\|²⟩ = Σ\|p⃗\|² for isotropic gamma directions. The modes differ only in shape.

Two findings worth carrying to the paper:

1. **The decay-in-flight ambiguity is negligible after folding.** On the recoil axis the slow
   (74 discrete lines, 182-671 eV, **none below 100 eV**) and fast (continuous) limits look
   completely different, but the folded RoI contributions are **3.334 vs 3.329** counts/kg/day —
   a **0.2%** spread. The observable does not resolve the regime question.

2. **The fold, not the recoil-axis shape, sets the contribution.** Only 1.6% of resolved captures
   recoil into 10-100 eV_nr (the spectrum peaks at ~321 eV, above the RoI), but E_rec ≈ 0.5 E_dep
   drags the 100-700 eV peak down into the 10-100 eV reconstructed band. Reading the contribution
   off the recoil axis would understate it ~17×.

Budget impact (10-100 eV RoI, /100 suppression):

| term | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| capture, single bound | 43.998 | — |
| capture RESOLVED (spectrum, 26.85%) | — | **3.334** |
| capture UNRESOLVED (73.15%, bound) | — | 32.184 |
| **S/B (est.)** | 1.334 | **1.257** |
| **S/B (incl. bounds)** | 0.729 | **0.797** |

The estimate drops because capture moved from bounds-only into the estimate; the bounded figure
rises because the shapeless term shrank from 44.0 to 32.2.

## The caveat that must travel with this

The table resolves **26.85%** of captures; the remaining 73.15% is the unresolved quasi-continuum
and is **never renormalised into the spectrum**. Doing so would assert the unresolved captures
recoil like the resolved ones — and because recoil goes as E², that is unsafe in the *unhelpful*
direction: unresolved cascades are higher-multiplicity, hence lower Σ E², hence *lower* recoil,
which moves them **toward and into** the RoI. The unresolved remainder is therefore carried as a
bound, and it is now the dominant term in this channel.
